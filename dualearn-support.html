DuaLearn Support

Need help with DuaLearn? Try the steps below or contact us by email.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Notifications and reminders

  • Daily and streak reminders are optional — turn them on in DuaLearn's settings and pick reminder times that suit you.
  • If reminders do not arrive, check that notification permission is granted for DuaLearn in your device settings.
  • Reminders are generated locally on your device; no internet connection or account is needed.

Your progress and data

  • All DuaLearn data — learning progress, streaks, bookmarks, dhikr history, prayer tracker and settings — is stored locally on your device.
  • There is no account and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes your progress, so only do that when you are happy to start fresh.
  • DuaLearn contains no advertisements and no in-app purchases.

Settings and accessibility

  • Language, light/dark theme, haptic feedback and sound effects can all be changed in the app's settings.
  • Accessibility options include text scaling, high contrast mode, screen reader optimization and reduce motion.
  • Set your daily Noor goal in settings to keep progress motivating rather than demanding.

Donations

  • The optional donation link opens PayPal in your browser and is governed by PayPal's terms and privacy policy.
  • Donating is never required to use any part of DuaLearn.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, DuaLearn version, language, and a short description of the problem. Screenshots are helpful, but remove personal information first.

Email DuaLearn support

Privacy

DuaLearn stores all personal data locally on your device and never sends it to a server. Read the DuaLearn Privacy Policy for details.

StrayFinder Support

Help with your account, posts, messages, privacy, safety reports, and technical issues.

Contact support

Email nuurcompass@gmail.com. Include “StrayFinder” in the subject, describe what happened, and provide your device platform and app version when relevant. Do not email one-time login codes or unnecessary sensitive information.

Email StrayFinder support

Safety and abusive content

Use the Report option on a post or profile so the relevant item is attached to your report. You can block a user from their profile or conversation. If a person or animal is in immediate danger, contact local emergency, animal-welfare, shelter, veterinary, or law-enforcement services; StrayFinder is not an emergency service.

Sign-in issues

Check the email address carefully, wait briefly for the six-digit code, and check spam or junk folders. Codes expire and can only be attempted a limited number of times. Request a new code if the previous one expired. Never share a login code.

Account and data

You can edit your profile, manage notifications and advertising consent, clear local cache, and delete your account from Settings. If you cannot access the app, follow the external account-deletion instructions.

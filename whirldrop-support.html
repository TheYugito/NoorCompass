Whirldrop Support

Need help with Whirldrop? Try the steps below or contact us by email.

Online play

  • Confirm your device has a working internet connection.
  • Update Whirldrop to the latest available version.
  • Close and reopen the app, then try matchmaking again.
  • If a match disconnects, include what happened and the approximate time in your support request.

Account and progress

  • Anonymous progress belongs to the device unless you link an email account in Whirldrop.
  • To restore linked progress, sign in using the same email account.
  • Never send us your password or a verification code.

Sound, reminders and ads

  • Check Whirldrop's sound setting and your device volume.
  • Daily-reward reminders require notification permission in your device settings.
  • Rewarded ads require an internet connection and may not always be available.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating-system version, Whirldrop version, language, and a short description of the problem. Screenshots are helpful, but remove personal information first.
